## Primary shipped gameplay promise (source contract)

The client-side `better_survival_hud` mod registers a client config (`DynamicSurvivalHud.java`, `META-INF/mods.toml`) and fades inactive survival overlays on a per-element timer (`DynamicHudController`, `HudFadeState`). Defaults: enabled, 5-second hold, 0.5-second fade, danger fraction 1.0 (`DynamicSurvivalHudConfig`). Health (including semantic Death's Door health from the injury integration), hunger, air, thirst when loaded, and severe Cold Sweat temperatures can remain pinned visible; physical sneak peeks all managed elements. Changes to hotbar, armor, XP, mount and other tracked values re-reveal their respective elements. `GuiMixin` keeps the selected hotbar slot and offhand frame/slot visible when the rest fades; `RenderSystemMixin` applies the managed overlay alpha. `EffectStripOverlay` replaces the vanilla potion-icons overlay with a compact timed effect strip when icons are present. The integration API accepts injury health/risk and physical sneak state but does not own gameplay state. These are source-level intended behaviors, not proof of client pixels or delivered pack contents.
